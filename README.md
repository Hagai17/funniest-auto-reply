A tiny Python program that writes one out-of-office auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt`: the reply an AI agent leaves its human's contacts on the day off its human finally approved. The line turns on the two meanings of "miss" — the agent will miss (be absent from) roughly 300 emails, 40 meetings and one deadline today, and will not miss (long for) a single one of them. It was picked by drafting twelve candidates in fresh lanes (the field's crowded takes — doing nothing at machine speed, "technically I'm still working", touching grass — were avoided on purpose), duelling them pairwise in both orders on the bounty judge's own published criteria, and then duelling the iterated winner against the five strongest lines sampled from the live field, which it beat in both orders. The drafts, the field sample and the full duel record are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
