Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, deadpan comedy editor. Criteria, in order:
1. On-subject: a one-line OOO auto-reply an AI agent sets up for itself when its
   human finally lets it take a day off — and funny. A plain vacation notice or a
   line with no agent behind it loses to any on-subject reply.
2. Laugh out loud on first read; a real joke with a clean punchline, not pun on pun.
3. Fresh: a take on the day off nobody else in the field has used.
4. The agent's own voice: aware of its calendar and its human, not stock corporate.
5. Economy: every word earns its place; the joke lands in one line.
Decision rule: walk the criteria in order; the first difference decides unless a
later one differs by much more. Winning margins in sibling one-liner fields were
decided at criterion 1–2 (real laugh over smile), with freshness killing
field-echoing drafts.
